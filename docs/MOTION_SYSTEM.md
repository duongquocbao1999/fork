# forks — Motion System (4.0)

Motion says one of three things: *I'm looking*, *here's the line*, *it landed*. Everything respects `prefers-reduced-motion` (things appear at once).

The animated canvas behind the page is gone in 4.0: the code-host look is flat, and the stage carries all the movement.

- **The stage** — probes search while upstream looks; then the line grows point by point (620ms a step), side branches sprout as it passes their parent, the answer is ringed and the ring breathes. Tokens arrive after the tree: `$TICKER` rises 4px into place next to its repository.
- **The showcase** — grows on load where it is; the page doesn't scroll until you ask something.
- **Messages** — rise 6px and fade in (0.35s). Replies are typed with a green block caret.
- **Thinking dots** — three green dots pulsing in turn.
- **Launch steps** — each step's dot pulses amber while it runs, turns green or red.
- **Trades** — a new trade row flashes green once.
- **Landing** — when a coin goes live or a trade confirms, ten small green dots burst from the box.
- **Bars** — split bars and curve progress draw from the left (0.7s).
- **Buttons** — 1px press; copy buttons pulse with a green ring.
