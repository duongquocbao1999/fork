Launch a repository's coin

One coin per repository, on pump.fun, from your own wallet. Its creator fees are split once and locked: the people who wrote the code, the repositories it forked from, and forks.

How it works

  1. Pick a public repository. The server reads its owner, top contributors and the repositories it forked from.
  2. Check the split. It is locked on-chain in the same bundle that creates the coin. Nobody can change it later, forks included.
  3. Name it, sign once. Your wallet signs 3–4 transactions; they land together on Solana or not at all.
  4. People claim on pump.fun by signing in with GitHub. They don't need to do anything first.

The split

Good to know

One coin per repository, a few repositories a day per wallet. Organizations and bots are never paid: an organization's part goes to its top people. Owners who don't want coins on their code can ask to be left out.

The authors of the code didn't make or endorse these coins. Not financial advice.
